# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The landing page is written first for IS463 assessors on demo day: people judging the prototype in a
short session, usually on a laptop or projector with a phone in hand. They need to understand the
loop and try each part within seconds.

Behind them are the people the product serves: pantry staff and cleaners logging drop-offs, OCIS and
facilities teams watching the bins, and SMU students and staff using the Grounds to Glow kiosk.

## Product Purpose

SMU Circular Coffee follows spent coffee grounds through one loop: pantry staff weigh each drop-off,
smart bin OCR sends the reading to a live dashboard, and the Grounds to Glow kiosk turns grounds into
a body scrub with an AI guide on hand. Success on demo day is an assessor completing that loop
themselves, and seeing their reading arrive on the page.

## Brand Personality

Practical, proven, hands-on. Confident about the operational detail, warm about the coffee. The page
should make people want to try the thing rather than read about it.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: gradient text, big-number stat grids, identical icon cards.
- University news-portal layouts where the product is buried under news, events and socials.
- Carousels that hide most of the story behind a timer.

## Design Principles

1. Show the working thing. Real data and real components beat descriptions of them.
2. The order on the page is the order of the loop.
3. Every section ends in something to try.
4. Keep the SMU identity: Oswald and Libre Franklin, navy, gold and limestone, angled planes.
5. Claim only what can be backed up. No invented figures or "verified" badges.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard use, visible focus and `prefers-reduced-motion` support. Live updates
are announced politely, and no information relies on colour alone.
